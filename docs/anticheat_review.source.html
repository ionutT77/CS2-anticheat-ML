What the original LSTM learned

Ionuț’s two-layer LSTM is a reasonable starting architecture. Normalization uses training data, and each publisher record’s engagements stay together. The journal’s best accuracy run gives the following comparison.

The AUC shows a useful ranking signal. At the default threshold, reported precision is 24.9%, so roughly three quarters of flags are false. Balanced sampling combined with a positive loss weight pushes decisions toward the cheating class; the threshold needs calibration.

Revising the same architecture

We grouped overlapping records, excluded 30 records with contradictory labels and separated training, validation, calibration and test data. The LSTM retains its 120,897 parameters. Revised training uses natural sampling, unweighted loss, compressed inputs and a separately calibrated threshold.

The split needs attention

The published split places 699 of 1,800 test records in trajectory groups shared with training; 25 are exact duplicates. Their effect on AUC is unknown. Missing published weights and predictions prevent exact replay.

The improvement is modest

On the audited split, engagement AUC rises from 0.742 to 0.750. Pooling 30 engagements raises record AUC. Differences between the record models remain uncertain; the LSTM supplement uses a previously examined test set.

ROC-AUC is the probability that a positive-label example ranks above a negative-label example, with ties counting as half. A constant score has AUC 0.5. Accuracy and precision also depend on the threshold and class mixture.

A richer CS2 benchmark

The CS2 experiment adds movement, distance, weapon and recoil context to aim and firing, then combines encounters into one assessment per player and match.

0.973

Test ROC-AUC
95% interval: 0.956 to 0.985

We fetched and verified 52.6 GB from 795 matches, extracting 141,633 encounters with 39 channels. Each input covers four seconds before the first bullet hit. The victim is identified retrospectively from that hit.

Test set: 1,130 eligible player-matches across 120 held-out matches, including 214 positive labels. Model selection uses validation data; calibration and thresholds use a separate split.

The selected model combines 75% temporal CNN and 25% boosted trees. Within manually reviewed matches, AUC is 0.971, supporting a signal beyond collection-source membership. Real-player separation across matches cannot be verified.

Thresholds and false flags

At the threshold chosen for accuracy, the model reaches 93.0%, compared with the 81.1% non-cheater baseline: 79 errors instead of 214. A stricter threshold reduces false flags and misses more positive-label cases.

Where to take it next

The next evaluation should establish who is being scored, how much gameplay the model sees and how confidently the labels describe cheating.

Independent data

Collect verified labels, stable pseudonymous player IDs across matches, skilled legitimate players and known cheat activation intervals. Reserve a separate recording source and future matches for testing. CS2CD remaps identities per match, and some negative labels are unreviewed.

Observation period

AUC rises from 0.834 after one eligible encounter to 0.946 after up to five and 0.973 over the match. These counts do not measure elapsed detection time. Add miss-only encounters and assess the players currently excluded: coverage is 94.5% of the release.

Feature ablations

Input-command mouse fields exist in CS2CD and are a useful next experiment. Exact visibility and audibility require faithful reconstruction or new recordings. Retain strong tree and LSTM baselines. More channels helped trees clearly here; the overall LSTM feature gain is uncertain.

Sequence pretraining and LLMs

The numerical patch Transformer reached 0.946 AUC. Masked pretraining on gameplay sequences is worth testing. No text LLM was fine-tuned. An LLM could help summarize telemetry for human review, with every claim tied to recorded evidence.

Presenting the thesis

Keep the LSTM as the reference, report calibration-selected thresholds and false-positive counts, and reserve a new final holdout. The current results do not establish an information ceiling or rule out overfitting. The code and journal provide a useful foundation.

The accompanying repository

CS2-anticheat-ML retains the original prototype. The additions in research/ provide extraction, training, calibration, saved models, predictions and reproducible experiments. Tests and a runnable example are included. README.md introduces the project; ADDITIONS.txt maps the files.
